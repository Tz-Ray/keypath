## Corresponding source

The derived data can be regenerated from its sources:

1. the upstream files above, pinned by URL, commit and sha256 in
   [`tables/VERSIONS.md`](https://github.com/Tz-Ray/cipher-project/blob/v2.0/tables/VERSIONS.md)
   of the KeyPath repository at tag `v2.0`;
2. KeyPath's `scripts/fetch_raw.py` (download) and
   `scripts/build_tables.py` (the processed tables under `tables/`), at
   the same tag;
3. this repository's `tools/build_data.py` (the files under `data/` and
   `tests/fixtures/`) and `tools/build_fonts.py` (the files under
   `fonts/`); see `README.md` for the commands.
