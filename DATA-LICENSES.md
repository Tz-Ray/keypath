## Changes to the GPL and LGPL sources

The files derived from the four copyleft sources are modified versions of
them, not copies; do not report errors in them upstream. Each directory
that holds such files has a `NOTICE` saying so. The KeyPath project made
these changes; the dates are those of the processed tables (pinned in
[`docs/VERSIONS.md`](docs/VERSIONS.md)) and of this site's files (the
repository's history records any later change).

- **SKK-JISYO.L** → `data/en/ja_romaji/`, `data/ja/`, `tests/fixtures/`. Changed
  2026-07-10: converted from EUC-JIS-2004 to UTF-8; the header, comments
  and the okuri-ari section removed; entries whose reading does not
  round-trip through KeyPath's romaji table, and candidates that are Lisp
  expressions or contain tabs, removed; `;` annotations removed. Changed
  2026-09-24: for each word of a 10,000-word English list, one JMdict
  translation that is also an SKK candidate is kept, with its first
  reading typed as romaji, its position among that reading's candidates,
  the number of candidates and the first four; stored as one JSON file per
  initial letter. Changed 2026-09-29: in `tests/fixtures/`, readings also
  typed as JIS kana keystrokes; in `data/ja/lists.json`, the whole
  candidate lists of eleven Japanese example readings (an empty list for a
  reading with none), in one JSON file.
- **FreeDict spa-eng 0.3.1** → `data/en/es_accent/`,
  `data/challenges/02.json`, `tests/fixtures/`. Changed 2026-07-10: TEI
  markup and header removed, keeping each headword and its translations;
  Unicode NFC, lowercased, whitespace collapsed; duplicate headwords and
  translations, and those containing `/` or tabs, removed; translations
  reordered by English word frequency (wordfreq); a Spanish frequency
  added. Changed 2026-09-24: indexed from English to Spanish; for each
  English list word, the first Spanish headword that translates it is

- **libchewing-data** (`word.csv`, `tsi.csv`) → `data/zh/`,

## Form of distribution

The files under `data/` and `tests/fixtures/` are distributed only as
complete, unobfuscated UTF-8 JSON and plain text (the fixtures gzipped),
readable and editable in any text editor; no compiled form of them is
distributed. The unmodified upstream works are public, pinned by URL,
commit and sha256 in [`docs/VERSIONS.md`](docs/VERSIONS.md), a
byte-for-byte copy of KeyPath's `tables/VERSIONS.md` at tag `v2.5` (its
sha256 is the tables edition above). The programs that produced these files
from them are separate works that do not include the upstream dictionaries:
KeyPath's own table builder, which is not published, and this repository's
`tools/build_data.py`, which needs it. Neither is needed to study, change
or redistribute the data. The fonts are regenerated by this repository's
`tools/build_fonts.py` from the pinned upstream font.
