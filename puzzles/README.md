# The six KeyPath puzzles

Play them on the [KeyPath page](https://tz-ray.github.io/keypath/#challenges): each is a ciphertext with no key.
Work out the keyboard, read the keys, and pick the words that make sense. All keyboards are PC layouts. Answers ignore spaces, punctuation and capitals.

| # | level | title | hint | keyboards | files |
|---|-------|-------|------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Warm-up | Type it like a local | Two characters of very common courtesy. 🇹🇼⌨️ |  | [`challenge-01/`](challenge-01/) |
| 2 | Warm-up | Dos teclados, un mensaje | No keyboard picture this time, and the digits do not all mean the same thing. | Two keyboards | [`challenge-02/`](challenge-02/) |
| 3 | Easy | The Key Right of the Space Bar | Nobody pressed the key to the right of the space bar. |  | [`challenge-03/`](challenge-03/) |
| 4 | Medium | Mother Tongue | Открытка из Москвы, typed on a PC. Every word but one; that one insisted on being typed in its mother tongue. | Two keyboards | [`challenge-04/`](challenge-04/) |
| 5 | Hard | A Friend from Afar | The Master said it. Seoul recites it. One word came from even farther away. | Three keyboards | [`challenge-05/`](challenge-05/) |
| 6 | Expert | Each in Its Own Way | …несчастлива по-своему. | Four keyboards | [`challenge-06/`](challenge-06/) |

Each folder holds the puzzle's `ciphertext.txt` and, as **spoilers**, the author's
`key.json`, the `plaintext.txt` and `solve-path.md`, the setter's way to crack it.
A solve path may also give away steps of later puzzles.
